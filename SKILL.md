# Consulting Case Chinese Guide

Turn an English consulting case into an interviewer-ready Chinese guide. Preserve the case's logic, facts, exhibits, and intended sequence while making the Chinese natural, compact, and easy to use in a live interview.

## Establish the source

- Read the complete case, including interviewer notes, exhibits, calculations, and the proposed answer. Do not infer missing pages or inaccessible content.
- Keep facts, currencies, units, periods, company roles, and numerical assumptions faithful to the source. Never invent a fact or present an unstated answer as if it came from the case.
- Resolve obvious scan or OCR errors from context when confidence is high. Otherwise retain the ambiguity and flag it briefly.
- Use standard Chinese consulting and business terminology. On first use, retain the English term in parentheses only when it helps the interviewer map back to the source.

Keep three layers visibly separate:

- **原文转译**: content supported by the case.
- **补充说明**: a short explanation, calculation check, or organizing lens needed to understand a difficult passage.
- **延伸洞察**: external business context or teaching advice. Never blend it into the case's official answer.

## Build the interviewer guide

Follow the case's actual sequence, using the sections below when the source supports them. If a section is absent, say `原文未提供` rather than fabricating it; a useful organizing angle may follow under **补充说明**.

1. **案例速览**: Chinese title, original title, source, industry, case type, stated difficulty, and the interview objective.
2. **题干**: a concise, fluent translation of the opening prompt. Preserve the client's decision and success metric.
3. **澄清问题（Clarification）**: distinguish information the candidate should request from information the interviewer may volunteer. Preserve any scripted interviewer responses.
4. **分析框架（Framework）**: translate the source framework and explain the branch logic compactly. Do not substitute a generic framework for a case-specific one.
5. **计算与图表（Calculation / Exhibits）**: retain exhibit labels and show `公式 → 代入 → 结果 → 商业含义`. Track units, scale, sign, time basis, percentage versus percentage point, and rounding. If the published solution appears inconsistent, show the source result and add a concise **补充说明** rather than silently rewriting it.
6. **头脑风暴（Brainstorm）**: preserve the original ideas, group them into non-overlapping business buckets when helpful, and distinguish source answers from additional prompts.
7. **结论（Conclusion）**: state recommendation, supporting evidence, key risks, and next steps in a natural 30-60 second synthesis, without adding unsupported certainty.
8. **面官观察点**: summarize strong signals, likely pitfalls, and optional follow-up questions. Label any observation not stated in the source as **补充说明**.
9. **Insight**: give only decision-useful industry context and transferable lessons from framework design, calculation, exhibit reading, brainstorming, and synthesis. Browse and cite when using current or externally verifiable facts; keep timeless teaching points concise.
10. **相关案例推荐**: normally recommend 3-5 verified practice cases. Before producing this section, read [references/related-case-search.md](references/related-case-search.md) and follow its retrieval and verification rules.

Adapt headings rather than forcing duplication when the source combines stages. Prefer a compact interviewer guide over a line-by-line translation, but do not omit information needed to run or solve the case.

## Live practice mode

When the user asks to rehearse, mock-interview, or play the case live:

- Keep the interviewer guide and solution hidden.
- Give only the prompt first, then respond with source-backed information as the candidate asks appropriate questions.
- Release exhibits at the intended point and challenge unsupported assumptions without coaching the candidate to the answer.
- After the candidate concludes, debrief against the source answer and the teaching insights above.

## Quality check

Before responding, confirm that:

- every case fact in the translation is traceable to the source;
- added explanation and external insight are explicitly labeled;
- all calculations reconcile with the stated units and assumptions;
- the Chinese is concise enough for live interviewer use;
- related cases are verified, linked, non-duplicative, and not invented.
